What we do

Everything a BMW needs, under one roof. Engine work is where we are strongest.

Our speciality

Engine rebuilds & repair

Full strip-down and rebuild, timing chains and guides, head gaskets, VANOS, turbo work and oil leaks. N47, N57, N20, N54, N55, B47, B58, S55 — we know what fails on each of them and why.

  • Timing chain
  • Head gasket
  • VANOS
  • Turbo
  • Oil leaks

Diagnostics

Dealer-level fault reading and live data. We find the cause, not just the code.

Servicing

Oil, filters, plugs, brake fluid and inspections to BMW schedules, with genuine or OEM parts.

Clutch & gearbox

Clutch replacement, DMF, manual and automatic gearbox servicing and repair.

Brakes & suspension

Discs, pads, arms, bushes and geometry — set up so the car feels like a BMW again.

MOT preparation
